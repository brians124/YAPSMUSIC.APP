            Help & Tips
            Can I use my phone?

            Yes. YAPS Studio opens right inside the app, so a phone is all you need to start. Headphones help you hear your mix clearly.

            Do I need internet?

            The first time you open the Studio it needs a connection to load. After that, mixes you save stay on your device.

            Where are my mixes saved?

            Saved mixes stay on your device and show under My Saved Mixes on Home, where you can play them any time. They only become public when you publish them.

            How do I share my music with everyone?

            Tap Publish to YAPS to open the upload page. Publishing is for artist accounts, so you may be asked to switch to one first.

            What equipment do I need to record?

            Your phone’s microphone works to begin with. For a cleaner sound, plug in headphones and record somewhere quiet.

            Overview

            YAPSMusic ("YAPSMusic", "we", "us") is a music streaming and discovery platform built for Uganda and the wider region. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the app, how we use it, and the choices you have.

            Information We Collect

            Account information name, email address, and password (stored securely, never in plain text) when you create an account.

            Usage data, songs you play, likes, follows, playlists, search history, and listening activity, used to power your Library, recommendations, and charts.

            Artist & upload data if you use the Artist Dashboard, we collect the tracks, artwork, and metadata you upload, along with basic performance stats (plays, likes) for your releases.

            Device & local storage preferences like theme, language, audio quality, and offline/downloaded content are stored locally on your device (browser localStorage), not on our servers.

            Tipping data the tipping feature (MTN MoMo / Airtel Money) does not process payments. A person who wants to tip pays the artist directly from their own mobile money account. YAPSMusic only shows the number the artist chose to share with signed-in users and records that a tip was announced. YAPS never collects PINs, card numbers or account balances.

            How We Use Your Information

            We use your information to operate and improve YAPSMusic: to play and recommend music, maintain your library and playlists, power artist tools, keep your account secure, and communicate important updates. We do not sell your personal information.

            Third-Party Services

            YAPSMusic relies on a small number of third-party services to function: Supabase for account authentication, database, and file storage; YouTube for embedded video playback where available; and standard content-delivery services for fonts and icons. These providers process data only as needed to deliver those features.

            Your Choices

            You can update your profile, clear locally-stored preferences from Settings, and request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting support. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser data removes locally-stored information from your device.

            Children's Privacy

            YAPSMusic is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age.
